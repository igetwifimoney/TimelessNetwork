Questions, Answered

No. Everything shared in Timeless Trading is educational. Signals and breakdowns are ideas, not instructions — you trade at your own risk and make your own decisions.
I'm a complete beginner — is this for me?
Yes. Start with Free or Starter to learn the mindset and system at your own pace, then step up to V.I.P for live calls and real-time signals when you're ready.
What's the difference between Starter and V.I.P?
Starter gives you the course library, weekly recaps, and pinned ideas. V.I.P adds live trading calls, real-time signals, insider wallet access, and 24/7 priority support.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Memberships are billed monthly and you can cancel anytime — no lock-in.
How does the 1-on-1 mentorship work?
It's a one-time program with direct sessions, a personalized trading plan, and ongoing feedback from the founders. Spots are limited each month and every application is reviewed personally.
